Google Analytics (GA4), Meta Pixel, and other tracking tools give you endless data — but which numbers actually matter? 
 
Here are **12 core events** every small-business website should monitor to make data-driven decisions that boost revenue.

---

### 🧭 1. Page View Depth  
See how far visitors go beyond your homepage. A high drop-off after one page means your internal links or CTAs may need work.

### ✉️ 2. Contact Form Submission  
Track every time someone fills out your form — your best indicator of conversion.

### 📞 3. Phone Click (Tap to Call)  
Essential for service businesses. Add 'onclick' tracking to call buttons so you know how many leads start from mobile.

### 💬 4. Live Chat Interactions  
If you use chat widgets, track engagement volume — it’s a strong signal of buying intent.

### 🛒 5. Add to Cart / Quote Request  
Even if they don’t purchase, these show product or service interest.  

### 💳 6. Completed Checkout / Payment  
Your ultimate success metric. Tag it with revenue value in GA4.

### 📧 7. Newsletter Signup  
Measure top-funnel lead capture and campaign ROI.

### 🎥 8. Video Play or 25% / 50% / 100% Completion  
Gauge how well video content keeps attention.

### 🧭 9. Outbound Link Clicks  
Track clicks to partner sites, social profiles, or affiliate offers — helpful for sponsorship analytics.

### 🔍 10. Site Search Queries  
What users type into your search bar reveals content gaps and intent.

### ⭐ 11. Scroll Depth (75%)  
Know whether visitors reach the CTA or pricing sections before bouncing.

### 🧠 12. File Downloads  
Track PDF guides, pricing sheets, or portfolios to identify high-interest topics.

---

### 🧩 Implementation Tips

- Use **Google Tag Manager** to configure these without altering code each time.  
- Verify events in **GA4 > Events > Realtime** to confirm they fire properly.  
- Set up **Goals or Conversions** for your top three events.  

---

### 📊 Turn Data → Decisions

- Low scroll depth? Simplify your layout.  
- High form starts but low submissions? Shorten your form fields.  
- Strong video completion? Use that format in ads.  

Analytics isn’t about numbers — it’s about **actionable insight**.

---

Tracking these 12 events ensures you’re measuring **what actually matters** — behavior that leads to business growth.
