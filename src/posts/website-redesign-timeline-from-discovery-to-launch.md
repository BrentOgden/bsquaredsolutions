Redesigning a website is rarely “just swapping out pages.” A successful redesign touches strategy, content, design, development, SEO, and launch planning. Knowing what happens *when* helps you budget correctly, avoid surprises, and keep stakeholders aligned.

Below is a **realistic website redesign timeline**, based on how modern small-business projects actually run.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy (1–2 weeks)

This is the most skipped—and most important—phase.

### What happens:
- Business goals and success metrics defined
- Target audience and user journeys clarified
- Existing site audit (content, SEO, performance)
- Feature and scope definition

### Deliverables:
- Project roadmap
- Page list (sitemap)
- Technical requirements
- Content responsibilities

> Skipping discovery almost always leads to scope creep, delays, or a site that “looks nice” but doesn’t convert.

---

## Phase 2: Content & Architecture (1–3 weeks)

Design should never come before content.

### What happens:
- Final sitemap approved
- Page-level goals defined
- Copywriting or content revisions
- SEO keyword alignment per page

### Deliverables:
- Final page structure
- Draft or final copy
- SEO metadata outlines

If content is delayed, the entire project slows down.

---

## Phase 3: Visual Design (2–3 weeks)

This is where the site starts to feel real.

### What happens:
- Homepage design
- Key interior page layouts
- Mobile and responsive considerations
- Brand refinement (colors, spacing, typography)

### Deliverables:
- Design comps or prototypes
- Design system elements
- Stakeholder approval

Design revisions are normal—but unlimited revisions are a timeline killer.

---

## Phase 4: Development (2–4 weeks)

Designs become a working website.

### What happens:
- Front-end build (React, WordPress, etc.)
- CMS setup
- Forms, integrations, and animations
- Performance optimization

### Deliverables:
- Functional staging site
- Responsive layouts
- Integrated content

This phase often overlaps with final content cleanup.

---

## Phase 5: QA, SEO & Launch Prep (1–2 weeks)

Before launch, everything gets stress-tested.

### What happens:
- Cross-browser testing
- Mobile testing
- Page speed optimization
- SEO checks (titles, meta, redirects)
- Analytics setup

### Deliverables:
- Launch checklist
- Redirect map
- Final approval

---

## Phase 6: Launch & Post-Launch (1 week)

Launching isn’t the finish line.

### What happens:
- DNS updates
- Monitoring traffic and errors
- Bug fixes
- Post-launch improvements

Smart teams plan a **30-day post-launch refinement window**.

---

## Typical Redesign Timeline (At a Glance)

| Phase | Time |
|-----|-----|
| Discovery | 1–2 weeks |
| Content | 1–3 weeks |
| Design | 2–3 weeks |
| Development | 2–4 weeks |
| QA & Launch | 1–2 weeks |
| **Total** | **8–12 weeks** |

---

## Common Causes of Delays
- Late content delivery
- Too many stakeholders
- Undefined approval process
- Scope changes mid-project

---

## Final Thought

A website redesign succeeds when expectations are realistic. Clear phases, defined responsibilities, and structured approvals keep projects moving—and protect your budget.

If you want help scoping a redesign that stays on schedule, start with strategy before pixels.
